HOW I APPROACH PROBLEMS

Not just ideas.
A working process.

  1. UnderstandFigure out the business problem, the people involved and what success means.
  2. DesignChoose a simple strategy, clear workflow or useful product approach.
  3. BuildTurn the idea into something testable: a campaign, a website, an automation or a tool.
  4. MeasureLook for evidence, trade-offs and what actually changed.
  5. ImproveIterate, document lessons and keep the parts that work.
