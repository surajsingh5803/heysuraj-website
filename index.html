WHO / WHY / WHAT

I’m Suraj Kumar—a marketer, entrepreneur and independent builder who enjoys taking an idea from the first question to a working system. My path has moved through business education, banking, real estate, digital marketing, client consulting and software experiments. Those experiences shape how I think about growth: behind every metric is a person trying to do something.

In marketing, my work connects search visibility, paid acquisition, conversion journeys and reporting. I care about the next step after somebody clicks: whether the page helps, whether the enquiry is meaningful, and whether the business can follow up.

Outside client work, I experiment with AI assistants, an SEO audit engine, lead-research workflows, CRM systems and new content formats. Some are ventures, some are functioning prototypes, and others are ideas that taught me something. The status of each matters. This site documents the process, the decisions and what I learned—not just finished screenshots.

HOW I APPROACH PROBLEMS

Not just ideas.
A working process.

  1. UnderstandFigure out the business problem, the people involved and what success means.
  2. DesignChoose a simple strategy, clear workflow or useful product approach.
  3. BuildTurn the idea into something testable: a campaign, a website, an automation or a tool.
  4. MeasureLook for evidence, trade-offs and what actually changed.
  5. ImproveIterate, document lessons and keep the parts that work.
