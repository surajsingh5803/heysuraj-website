CLEAR EXPECTATIONS / NO SURPRISES

A tiny pet.
A clear promise.

Pico is here to spend time with visitors. Here's what happens to what you share, which optional services are active, and what remains under your control.

HEYSURAJ.COMUPDATED 14 NOVEMBER 2026

Pico is a fictional, interactive red panda on the personal website of Suraj Kumar. He is not a real animal or person. Conversations are meant for casual companionship and approved information about Suraj, not confidential advice.

What happens to our chat?

In the current standalone mode, Pico generates responses in your browser from prewritten conversational behaviour and approved facts. The most recent conversation turns and the visitor category you select are stored in your browser's sessionStorage so the conversation can continue between site pages in the same tab. They are not sent to Suraj or saved to the website's database unless you separately choose to submit a message. Use Pico's Clear chat control to delete that session history at any time. Closing the tab normally clears session storage.

When the optional AI service is enabled in the future, a visitor's messages and limited recent context will be sent to a separate server and an AI model provider for a reply. The chat interface will disclose that mode. We will not describe a service as connected unless it actually is.

Voice, microphone, sounds and preferences

Pico's spoken replies and original ambient tones are optional and off by default. If you enable voice, browser speech synthesis produces the sound. If you tap the microphone, your browser may request microphone permission and use its speech-recognition service. Browser/provider handling varies; we do not intentionally save audio recordings on HeySuraj's static site.

Voice, sound, music, cursor-follow and quiet-mode choices are saved as simple settings in browser localStorage. No music or speech starts without a visitor's interaction and consent. Browsers can block audio until someone interacts with the page.

Leaving a message for Suraj

Leaving a note is optional. Pico asks for the note itself, an optional name, optional reply contact and explicit consent. In the standalone mode, the website prepares a WhatsApp draft addressed to Suraj's number. You must review and press Send inside WhatsApp to deliver it. WhatsApp processes communications under its own terms. Opening a draft does not mean the message was sent or stored by HeySuraj.

If a secure message service is later enabled, consented notes can be stored in a private database together with the visitor category, original page and submission time. Suraj can review them in a protected owner inbox and may receive an email alert. An email alert is not guaranteed simply because a database save succeeded. The system does not automatically add casual chat to the inbox.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can ignore Pico, close the conversation, switch on quiet mode, turn off sounds, clear the current-tab chat or choose not to submit a note. If you previously sent a note and would like it deleted from Suraj's private records, contact him through the published WhatsApp contact, identifying the note sufficiently for it to be found. Messages shared through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own deletion and retention controls.

Security and limitations

HeySuraj's website uses HTTPS via GitHub Pages. The optional private service is separate from the public website and must have restricted access, a private database, rate limits and authenticated ownership. API tokens and personal knowledge not explicitly approved for publication must not be placed in public browser code. Security measures reduce risk but no online transmission is risk-free.

We do not claim that Pico knows secret details about Suraj. Facts he shares are from approved, non-sensitive material. If information is missing, Pico should acknowledge it rather than invent personal details.

Questions?

You can contact Suraj via LinkedIn or WhatsApp. This page describes current client-side behaviour and the future optional service separately, so you can understand the difference.
